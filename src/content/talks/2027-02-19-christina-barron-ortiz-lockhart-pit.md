## Bio

Christina Barron-Ortiz is Assistant Curator of Quaternary Palaeontology at the Royal Alberta Museum (RAM). She received her PhD from the University of Calgary in 2016 and has been working at the RAM since 2015.

## Abstract

The Lockhart Pit, located approximately 75 km southeast of Hanna, Alberta, is a gravel pit operated intermittently since the 1980s. Staff from the Royal Alberta Museum and Special Areas Board first visited the pit in 2019 and happened to find a small, but significant, sample of fossils that suggested the gravel deposits and the fossils they preserve are unique and fill a gap in the fossil record of Alberta. Over the past five years, my colleagues and I have returned to this intriguing vertebrate locality to collect additional fossils and geological data that is helping us to better understand the age and depositional context of this vertebrate assemblage. In this presentation, I will discuss preliminary findings of our ongoing research at the Lockhart Pit.

The Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum is bringing fossil digging, fossil cleaning, and a fossil touch table to the Grande Prairie Public Library. Visitors can dig for fossils, learn to clean and identify them, and handle specimens from the museum collection.

**When:** Friday, November 20, 2026, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM  
**Where:** Grande Prairie Public Library, Grande Prairie, AB  
**Cost:** Free, all ages

Drop in anytime. No registration needed. The Grande Prairie Public Library is hosting the visit.

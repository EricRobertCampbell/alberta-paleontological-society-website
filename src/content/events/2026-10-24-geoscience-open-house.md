The Canmore Museum and the Canadian Rockies Earth Science Resource Centre present a free drop-in Geoscience Open House.

Visit exhibitor booths, bring your own rocks, minerals, or fossils for identification, and learn about the geology of the Bow Valley. Exhibitors include the Canadian Rockies Earth Science Resource Centre, the Canmore Museum, Earth Science for Society in Motion, the American Association of Petroleum Geologists, the Burgess Shale Geoscience Foundation, and the Archaeological Society of Alberta.

**When:** Saturday, October 24, 2026, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM  
**Where:** Canmore Civic Centre and CRESRC, Canmore, AB  
**Cost:** Free

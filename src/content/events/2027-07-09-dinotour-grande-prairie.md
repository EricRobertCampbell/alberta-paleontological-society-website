Dinotour 2027 is a four-day, family-oriented tour in Grande Prairie, Alberta, July 9–12, 2027. Proceeds support the Dinosaur Research Institute.

During the tour you will:

- Learn about the dinosaurs of northern Alberta from Dr. Philip Currie, Dr. Eva Koppelhus, Dr. Corwin Sullivan, and tour coordinator Mona Trick.
- Tour the Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum galleries, collection, and preparation areas, search for tiny fossils using microscopes, and prepare fossils in the museum laboratory.
- Dig dinosaur fossils from the Pipestone Creek Bone Bed with museum staff.
- Raft the Wapiti River with museum experts, see dinosaur footprints, and look for fossils at Red Willow River Falls.

**The tour includes:**

- Guided tour with bus transportation to and from Grande Prairie, Alberta
- 5 nights accommodation (double occupancy), including the night before the tour and each night of the tour
- All meals over the 4 days of the tour
- Admission to the Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum for all 4 days
- Fees for the Pipestone Creek Bone Bed excavation and the Wapiti River raft excursion
- Guidebook and goodie bag
- A Canadian charitable tax donation receipt for a portion of the fees

**Cost:** CDN $2,930.00 (including GST). Single supplement: CDN $387.25 (including GST).

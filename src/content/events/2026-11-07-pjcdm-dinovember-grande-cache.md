The Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum is bringing fossils to the Grande Cache Tourism & Interpretive Centre for an afternoon of fossil digging, fossil cleaning, and a fossil touch table.

**When:** Saturday, November 7, 2026, 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM  
**Where:** Grande Cache Tourism & Interpretive Centre, Grande Cache, AB  
**Cost:** Free, all ages

The Municipal District of Greenview and the Grande Cache Municipal Library helped make the afternoon possible.

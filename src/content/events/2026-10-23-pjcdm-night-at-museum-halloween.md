The dinosaurs never sleep. Will you?

This Halloween, kids ages 7 to 14 can spend the night in the gallery under towering skeletons, with flashlight adventures, spooky dino games, a not-too-scary movie, and Halloween themed snacks. After dark is when the adventures begin.

**When:** Friday, October 23 – Saturday, October 24, 2026  
**Drop-off:** 6:30 PM – 7:00 PM  
**Pick-up:** 9:30 AM  
**Where:** Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum, Wembley, AB

Spots are limited. Book on the [museum website](https://dinomuseum.ca/night-museum) or call 587-771-0662.

Say goodbye to SUE with 4.6 billion years of Earth science. This fourth annual Earth Science Weekend brings together the TELUS Spark team, the Burgess Shale Geoscience Foundation, Earth Science for Society, the Geological Survey of Canada, the Alberta Palaeontological Society, and the Royal Tyrrell Museum.

**When:** Saturday, November 21 – Sunday, November 22, 2026, 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM each day  
**Where:** TELUS Spark Science Centre, Calgary  
**Cost:** Included with general admission or a Spark membership

### Atrium activities (10 AM – 3 PM)

- **Burgess Shale Geoscience Foundation** — fossils from over 500 million years ago
- **Earth Science for Society** — hands-on geology, water, and energy
- **Geological Survey of Canada** — Guess the Rock or Fossil (Sunday only)
- **Alberta Palaeontological Society** — match real fossils to Earth's timeline
- **Royal Tyrrell Museum** — Alberta's prehistoric past
- **SUE Crowns** — decorate a dinosaur crown in honour of SUE

### Hands-on (included with admission)

- **Fossil Casting** in Steven's Lab (ages 7+; sign up on arrival; spots limited)
- **Jurassic X-Ray Tech** and **Fossil Texture Rubbings** in the Creative Kids Museum

### Inspiration Stage (same schedule both days)

- **All the Better to Eat You With! The Feeding Adaptations and Ecology of Tyrannosaurs** — 12:30 PM, with Dr. François Therrien, Curator of Dinosaur Palaeoecology at the Royal Tyrrell Museum of Palaeontology
- **What is Under Calgary?** — 2:30 PM, with paleontologist Cory Gross

SUE: The _T. rex_ Experience is included with admission and remains at Spark until November 29, 2026.

Prepare to be frightened in this live online presentation of the Royal Tyrrell Museum's top 10 scariest specimens. You will see a crocodile skull the size of a Smart car, the largest dinosaur claw ever found, and a creature that had even bigger teeth than T. rex. The program concludes with a Q&A session with a Museum expert.

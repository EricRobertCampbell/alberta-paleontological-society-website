Come to the museum this Pro-D day to learn about the living dinosaurs of planet Earth — how modern birds connect to ancient dinosaurs.

**When:** Monday, November 30, 2026, 9:30 AM – 2:30 PM  
**Where:** Tumbler Ridge Museum, Tumbler Ridge, BC  
**Ages:** 6–10  
**Cost:** $30 per person, or free for Tumbler Ridge youth

A CIBC grant covers 100% of museum entry and program fees for TR youth and their caregivers. Bring proof of address in Tumbler Ridge to the museum to sign up for the free rate.

Sign up closes November 29. Register online at [trmf.ca](https://www.trmf.ca/events/camps) or at the museum front desk.

Questions? Call 250-242-3466 or email [programs@trmf.ca](mailto:programs@trmf.ca).

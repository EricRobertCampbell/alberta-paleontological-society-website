A free, family-friendly chance to have rocks, minerals, and fossils identified. Experts from the Geological Survey of Canada will be on hand, with hands-on specimens from Alberta and across Canada. The Alberta Palaeo Society will also be there. Made possible by Kumon Math & Reading Centre of Airdrie.

**When:** Saturday, November 21, 2026, 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM  
**Where:** Community Living Room, Airdrie Public Library, 805 Main Street South, Airdrie, AB  
**Cost:** Free  
**Audience:** All ages

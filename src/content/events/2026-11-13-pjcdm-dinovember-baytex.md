The Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum is bringing a fossil dig, fossil cleaning, and a fossil touch table to the Baytex Energy Centre in Peace River. Brushes, goggles, and fossils are provided.

**When:** Friday, November 13, 2026, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM  
**Where:** Baytex Energy Centre, Peace River, AB  
**Cost:** Free, all ages

Thanks to the Baytex Energy Centre and the Town of Peace River for another DINOvember collaboration.

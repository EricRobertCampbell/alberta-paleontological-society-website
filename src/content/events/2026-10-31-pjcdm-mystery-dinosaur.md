Something strange is happening at the museum. Visitors have reported mysterious sounds, strange footprints, and sightings of a dinosaur spirit roaming the halls after dark. An old museum legend says only true dinosaur enthusiasts can uncover its secret name and send it back to the prehistoric past.

This Halloween, become a Dino Detective. Follow the clues, solve the puzzles, and complete a museum-wide scavenger hunt. Collect hidden letters and discover the magic word before the spirit disappears. The day also includes Halloween treats, scary movies, hands-on dinosaur and fossil activities, and a chance to dance with a dinosaur. Come dressed in your Halloween best.

At 1:00 PM, the Costume Parade & Contest starts. Three prizes are up for grabs: Most Creative Costume, Staff Favorite, and Best Prehistoric Costume. There is no costume theme, so come as a detective, a dinosaur, a ghost, or anything you like. To join the parade, stop by the Front Desk to sign up and collect a numbered badge.

**When:** Saturday, October 31, 2026, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM  
**Parade:** 1:00 PM  
**Where:** Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum, 9301 112 Avenue, Wembley, AB  
**Cost:** Included with admission

Back by popular demand for a **second lecture**! World-renowned palaeontologist Professor Philip Currie returns.

**When:** Sunday, September 27, 2026, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM (doors at 5:30 PM)  
**Where:** Franks Community Pub, 8815 99 St NW, Edmonton, AB  
**Cost:** $28.41 CAD  

More than a century ago, Alberta became the epicentre of one of the greatest fossil discoveries in history. Beginning with the “Great Canadian Dinosaur Rush” in 1910, hundreds of spectacular dinosaur skeletons were unearthed from Alberta’s Badlands and shipped to museums around the world.

Join Dr. Philip Currie as he takes us through Alberta’s incredible dinosaur history — from early fossil expeditions and the creation of Dinosaur Provincial Park and the Royal Tyrrell Museum, to the “Dinosaur Renaissance” that changed how we understand dinosaur behaviour, evolution, and biology.

And the discoveries aren’t over. Alberta remains one of the world’s most important places for dinosaur research, with new species still being found and technology revealing incredible details about these ancient animals.

With more than 350 scientific papers, 175 popular articles, and 20 books to his name, Currie has spent decades uncovering the stories of dinosaurs around the world. He played a key role in developing the Royal Tyrrell Museum and was honoured with the opening of the Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum in 2015.

Come discover why Alberta continues to be at the forefront of palaeontology — and what secrets might still be waiting beneath our Badlands.

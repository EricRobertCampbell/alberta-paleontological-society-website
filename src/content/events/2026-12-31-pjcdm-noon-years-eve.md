Get ready to party like it's 70 million years ago at the Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum this New Year's Eve.

**When:** Thursday, December 31, 2026, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM  
**Where:** Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum, Wembley, AB

Balloon drop and bubble wrap pop, “mimosa” bar, crafts, and a fossil touch table.

**Tickets:** Adults $15; seniors/veterans $12; youth/student $8; family $42; 4 and under free. Tickets are available [online](https://dinomuseum.ca/events/noon-years-eve-party) and at the door.

A student-focused palaeontology conference in Saskatoon, organized by the University of Saskatchewan Palaeobiology Society.

Students from Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba are invited to register. Everyone (including professors) may bring a poster. Limited spots are open for graduate students to present a talk.

**When:** February 27–28, 2027  
**Where:** Museum of Natural Sciences, University of Saskatchewan, Saskatoon, SK

Tickets and further details are linked from the Palaeobiology Society Instagram bio / Rubric club page.

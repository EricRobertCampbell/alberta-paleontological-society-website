_T. rex_, raptors, and _Triceratops_—meet the icons of the Cretaceous. During this 45-minute webcast, you’ll be introduced to the most common dinosaurs from the Alberta badlands. Along the way, you’ll learn how fossils are unearthed, prepared, and stored at the Royal Tyrrell Museum. The program concludes with an opportunity to send questions to a Museum expert.

**When:** Wednesday, April 28, 2027, 6:00 PM – 6:45 PM MT  
**Where:** Online  
**Cost:** $8 per at-home learner/one connection ($25 per class up to 30 students)

Registration closes at 3:30 p.m. MT the week before the program. A recording is available for 72 hours after the live broadcast. No private bookings.

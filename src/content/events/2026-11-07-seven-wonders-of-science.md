What do auroras, dinosaurs, and bugs have in common? They're all part of Seven Wonders of Science, presented by the University of Calgary Faculty of Science as part of Petri Dish by Beakerhead.

Hear from seven University of Calgary researchers, ask questions during live Q&As, and explore hands-on activities, including a science-themed escape room, fossil excavation, dinosaur creation, and a test of strength against a lemur.

- Auroras: Mysteries of the Northern Lights, with Emma Spanswick
- The human hand: Are you stronger than a lemur?, with Edwin Dickinson
- Artificial intelligence, with Richard Zhao
- Dinosaurs: Would T. rex eat you?, with Jared Voris
- Bugs: How bugs work, with Jackie Lebenzon
- Biomedical engineering, with Emma Frago
- Oceans, with Rachel Lauer

**When:** Saturday, November 7, 2026, 12:30 PM – 5:00 PM  
**Where:** EEEL Building, 750 Campus Dr. NW, Calgary  
**Cost:** Free, all ages

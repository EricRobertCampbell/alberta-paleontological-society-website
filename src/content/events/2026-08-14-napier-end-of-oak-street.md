Could you survive being transported to the Mesozoic?

*The End of Oak Street* premieres at the Napier Theatre on Friday, August 14. The movie begins at 7:30 p.m., but doors will open at 6:30 p.m. Arrive early to hear a museum curator discuss dinosaur survival tips, and try to answer some trivia questions!

These activities are free with movie admission.

**When:** Friday, August 14, 2026  
**Doors:** 6:30 PM  
**Movie:** 7:30 PM  
**Where:** Napier Theatre, 20 3rd Ave East, Drumheller, AB  

After a mysterious cosmic event transports a suburban neighbourhood somewhere unknown, the Platt family must stick together to survive among prehistoric creatures.

More information is available on the [Napier Theatre website](https://www.napiertheatre.com/).

A guided walking tour of the rocks and fossils around the University of Alberta North Campus and the river valley.

**When:** Sunday, October 18, 2026, 1:00 PM – about 2:00 PM  
**Where:** Meet in the Students' Union Building (SUB), between the Daily Grind and the glass doors to the Horowitz Theatre  
**Topics:** Urban geology, the river valley, and fossils

Join the Edmonton Geological Society for a tour of U of A North Campus geology and fossils.

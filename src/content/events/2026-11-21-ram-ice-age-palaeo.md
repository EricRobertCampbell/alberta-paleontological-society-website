Palaeontologists study fossils to uncover what life was like thousands of years ago. Become a junior palaeontologist and learn about what animals lived in Alberta during the Ice Age.

Get hands-on with real Ice Age fossils and learn the skills that help palaeontologists make their discoveries. Learn how to tell the difference between Ice Age fossils and how to piece together Alberta’s frozen past.

This workshop is for youth in Grades 4–8 (ages 9–14). No previous experience is required. Space is limited.

This is a drop-off activity. Parents cannot accompany participants during the workshop.

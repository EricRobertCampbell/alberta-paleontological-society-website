**Call for Entries: Paleo 2027 Logo Contest!**

The Alberta Palaeontological Society is excited to announce a logo design contest for Paleo 2027, our annual symposium bringing together amateur and professional palaeontologists. We're looking for a creative logo that will represent the symposium and capture the spirit of palaeontology in Alberta!

**Design Guidelines:**

- Must include the text "Paleo 2027"
- Should reflect palaeontology in Alberta - think fossils, dinosaurs, badlands, local geology, &c.

**Deadline:** Monday, November 9, 2026

**Prize:**

- A free one-year APS membership
- Your choice of an APS t-shirt or book
- Your winning design featured on all Paleo 2027 materials!

**How to Enter:**

Email your logo submission to president1@albertapaleo.org

The same details are on the [Paleo 2027 symposium page](/events/symposium/2027).

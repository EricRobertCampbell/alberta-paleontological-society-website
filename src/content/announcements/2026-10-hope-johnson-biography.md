Palaeontology in Alberta owes much to the legion of dedicated amateurs who scour the badlands for fossils and help with identification and discovery. One of the foremost of these was Hope Johnson (1916-2010), whose contributions to palaeontology are too numerous to mention. Darren Tanke, of the Royal Tyrrell Museum of Palaeontology, has generously made his biography of this amazing palaeontologist freely available to everyone.

If you want to see how palaeontology in Alberta has evolved and be inspired to go out and start looking, <a href="/openSourcePublications/Now%20There%20Was%20a%20Lady.pdf">give this a read!</a>

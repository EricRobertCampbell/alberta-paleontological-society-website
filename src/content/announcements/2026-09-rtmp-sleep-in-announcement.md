The Alberta Palaeontological Society is excited to invite you to a special overnight event at the Royal Tyrrell Museum of Palaeontology in Drumheller, Alberta!

**Date**: Friday, November 13 - Saturday, November 14

**Price**: $54 per person

**Registration Deadline**: Sunday, November 1

We’ve reserved 10 spots, with additional spots available on a first-come, first-served basis, so don't delay!

To sign up, fill out the [sign up form](/fieldTrips/2026/forms/rtmp-sleepin-signup.pdf). For additional details, see the [field trips page](/events/fieldtrips/).
